Wet wipes for personal hygiene are pre-moistened, single-use cloths that help you freshen up, clean your skin, and get rid of bacteria and sweat when water and soap are not available. A good cleansing wipe removes oil and odour-causing germs and bacteria from the skin more effectively than plain water. A good daily hygiene wipe is a quick and dependable way to stay clean during travelling with your kids, when delayed in traffic, or even at your desk after lunch.
