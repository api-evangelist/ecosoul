Sustainable office breakroom supplies are basic, everyday items that help offices replace traditional plastic disposable products with more environmentally friendly alternatives. Bagasse plates, compostable bowls, compostable cutlery, PLA cups, compostable hot cups , food containers, clamshells, tree-free toilet paper, kitchen towels, and napkins are some of these. Selecting eco-friendly office supplies doesn't have to be difficult.
