Reducing plastic in the kitchen does not mean changing everything at once. A few simple swaps can make everyday meals, food storage, and cleanup feel a little more sustainable. From reusable containers to tree-free kitchen paper towels and compostable tableware, there are many practical alternatives to common plastic products.
