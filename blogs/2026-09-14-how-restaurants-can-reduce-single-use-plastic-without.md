Single-use plastic is frequently used in daily operations at restaurants. Packaging, which includes everything from takeaway containers and clamshells to cups , cutlery, and food-service plates, is important in serving customers efficiently. However, choosing sustainable food packaging doesn't have to mean completely changing a restaurant's operations or paying higher costs.
