What Is Parents' Day? Hosting an Eco-Friendly Family Celebration Every fourth Sunday in July, the United States celebrates Parents' Day, which honours mothers, dads, and guardians together. It acknowledges the importance parents play in raising children and was founded in 1994 by President Bill Clinton.
