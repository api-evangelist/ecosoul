Millions of people drink coffee every day in disposable cups without even knowing what might happen to those cups. However, with the growing prominence of sustainability, a lot of people are wondering: “Are coffee cups compostable?” The solution is more complicated than it seems. Most cups are lined with plastic, which makes composting difficult, although some look like plain paper.
