In the United States, disposable plates are extremely popular for use in small backyard gatherings or in large-scale occasions. They are convenient, save on the time spent on cleaning up, and are perceived as a viable solution to a hectic lifestyle. However, the growing concern over the landfill has been increasing and has raised a significant question: Are disposable plates eco-friendly?
