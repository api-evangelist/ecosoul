How to use pet wipes is an important topic for modern pet parents, who want to keep their puppy clean and maintain hygiene without necessarily relying on regular bathing. Puppies are generally very curious and playful, and this can easily result in dirty paws, messy fur, and unwanted smells. However, compared to adult dogs, their skin is more sensitive & delicate, making traditional ways of cleaning too harsh for them.
