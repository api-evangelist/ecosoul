Disposable plates are useful when you are hosting a party, packing a meal, planning a picnic, or simply want an easier cleanup. But with so many options available, choosing the right plate can be confusing. You may see terms like compostable , biodegradable , bamboo , paper , and non-toxic disposable plates on product labels.
