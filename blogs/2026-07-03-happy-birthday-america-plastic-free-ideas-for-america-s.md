Happy Birthday America, for the 250th Anniversary (July 4th, 2026) can go plastic-free by using compostable, reusable, or natural alternatives to plastic plates, cups, and decorations. Little changes like using bagasse plates, cloth bunting, and birchwood cutlery make any cookout festive while reducing the amount of garbage that ends up in landfills during one of the nation's largest holidays. Key Takeaways On July 4th, 2026, America will celebrate the 4th of July in style as it marks its 250th birthday.
